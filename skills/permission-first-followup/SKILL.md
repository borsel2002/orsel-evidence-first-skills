# Permission-first follow-up
1. Confirm an existing relationship or recorded channel-specific consent.
2. Check the company reply ledger and opt-out list again immediately before any send.
3. Identify one segment bottleneck and offer a useful one-page procedure.
4. Identify the real company and sender. Include a working opt-out path.
5. Produce a draft only; a human approves and uses the existing sending gate.
Never clone a voice, invent a sender, or turn analytics consent into email consent.
